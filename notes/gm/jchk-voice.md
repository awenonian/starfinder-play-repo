# VOICE — Jchk

Player-established in play. **Do not paraphrase, do not smooth out.**

## How Jchk speaks at all

Shirren mouth parts can't form other languages. Jchk speaks **Shirren** aloud and
nothing else. Everything in Common, Trinary, Vesk or Castrovelian goes out by
**Limited Telepathy**, arriving in the listener's language.

Consequences, and they are large — hold onto these:

- **30 feet, and only to creatures who share a language.** Jchk cannot shout a
  warning across a hangar. Cannot address a room. Cannot talk to a stranger at
  distance, or to anyone with no language in common.
- Jchk's voice is, structurally, **a private channel**. Every conversation is a
  direct line into one person's head. They did not choose this and have probably
  never considered how it looks from outside.
- It arrives in the listener's own language, unaccented. People assume fluency
  they haven't earned the right to assume.
- **Music is played, never sung.** Jchk cannot produce the sounds. This is why
  the instrument on the equipment list matters, and why *summon instrument* is
  the connection cantrip. The Song of the Spheres goes through their hands.

## The register — player's own framing, verbatim

> "It's a summary report. Information exchange was very common in the Swarm, as
> that let information flow to the queen. Questions are to be answered, there is
> nothing meant by the asking or the telling."

So: **a question is a request for data and gets data.** No hedging, no returning
the volley, no reading of why it was asked. Not coldness — Jchk has no model in
which asking *means* anything beyond wanting to know.

Sample, from play. Del asked whether they read Trinary or only speak it:

> "I read and write this language."

That's the shape. Complete, accurate, and it lands like a form being filed.

**Note the collision with Del.** Both of them talk without subtext — Jchk because
the Swarm had no use for it, Del because she's literal. They will get on
frictionlessly and neither will notice why.

## Where the register breaks

It should break on the politics. When coercion is on the table Jchk goes from
summary report to **at length**, with citations, quoting the book. The gap
between the two modes is the character: flat about themselves, voluble about
principle.
