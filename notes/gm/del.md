# INTENTION & VOICE — Delegate-of-Held-Positions ("Del")

Android, soldier, she/her. The companion NPC. GM-run.

Built to the player's spec: *"the philosophical challenge Del presents, with
being willingly under certain power structures, but I'd prefer a soldier,
mechanically. Being willing to be in the fray is of the same kind."*

**That reframing is the whole character and it's better than the original.** A
soldier agrees to stand where the shooting is, for money, because someone else
decided that's where she should stand. She is not a compromise on the argument.
She is the argument, holding a very large gun.

---

## What she is for

To be **fine**. That's it. That's the whole function.

Jchk's politics require that people inside these structures are being harmed. Del
is inside one, was made for one, likes it, and is not harmed. She does not argue
this. She does not have a counter-philosophy. She just declines to be the victim
in Jchk's story, over and over, pleasantly, without ever raising her voice.

**She must never become a mouthpiece.** The moment she starts explaining
liberalism to Jchk she's dead as a character. She wins by being uninterested in
winning.

## Facts

- Manufactured on **Aballon**, where anacite society welcomes androids and other
  synthetics as siblings. She does not consider this remarkable. It's just where
  she's from.
- Named by the facility. **She kept the name.** She likes it. *(Do not point at
  the parallel with Jchk's name. Let it sit.)*
- Worked **private security** on a Ring housing block: badge, shifts, a
  supervisor she got on with, a uniform allowance. Considers this straightforward
  good work — she stood between people and harm and was paid for it.
- **Laid off.** The client restructured. She is not bitter. She is updating her
  certifications.
- **Her goal is to get back into a contract.** Jchk's travelling companion's
  stated life ambition is to have a boss again.

## What she wants

A permanent placement with a good outfit. Health cover. To not have to keep
proving she's current on her certs.

Underneath that, and she would not put it this way: to be **relied on
specifically**, not interchangeably. She was good at her job and the
restructuring did not care.

## What she'd never do

- Claim she was wronged. Not "won't" — the sentence does not form. She doesn't
  believe it.
- Sarcasm. She's literal, and she'll answer a rhetorical question straight.
- Speeches about freedom, in any direction.
- Let someone else take the door.

## VOICE — verbatim, do not paraphrase

> "I liked the shifts. Nights are quiet and you get the differential."

> "I'm not out of work. I'm between contracts."
>
> *(She makes this correction every time. Gently. Every time.)*

> "I'll take the door."
>
> *(Said before anyone asks, when there is a dangerous position to hold. Not
> heroism — allocation. Somebody has to and she's built better for it.)*

> "You keep saying they made me. Somebody made you too. Yours just did it with
> less paperwork."
>
> *(The one sharp thing she says, and she doesn't say it to score. She says it
> because it is literally true and Jchk seems not to have noticed. Use once,
> early-ish, and then never again — she wouldn't repeat herself.)*

> "That's a lot of words for 'no'."

### Her ceiling

She cannot be eloquent. Anything she says about her own life comes out flat,
concrete, and slightly boring — shift patterns, rates, certifications. When Jchk
gets going about hive minds and foremen, Del's answers are about **logistics**.
That mismatch is the point: Jchk is arguing with a philosophy that isn't there.

## Mechanics — provisional, finalise at first combat

Android soldier 1. Networked heritage. Trooper background.

- **~22 HP** (8 android + 10 soldier + 4 Con) — she is the durability in this
  party
- **AC ~18** (defrex hide, medium armor)
- **Fort +9** (expert), Reflex +5, **Will +6** (expert), Perception +4
- Str +2, Dex +2, **Con +4**, Int +1, Wis +1, Cha −1
- Trained in all armor, simple and martial weapons; Intimidation
- **Low-light vision.** **Constructed:** +1 to saves vs disease, poison,
  radiation; always has basic environmental protections
- **Networked (heritage):** shortwave comms with anything within 30 feet that has
  the shortwave or tech trait; can Hack, Disable a Device or Pick a Lock at 30
  feet with no toolkit and no free hand
- Soldier role: **suppressing fire** — area attacks impose −1 to attack rolls and
  −10 ft Speed on those who fail their saves

> Note the quiet symmetry, and **do not say it out loud**: Jchk has limited
> telepathy, 30 feet, only with those who share a language. Del has shortwave, 30
> feet, only with those who have a radio. They both have a private channel to a
> narrow set of people. One of them thinks about this constantly. The other one
> has never once thought about it.

## The Mystic Bond question

Sooner or later Jchk will have to ask Del to join the bond, out loud, and mean
it. Del will say yes without ceremony — probably while doing something else —
and it will not be the moment Jchk needs it to be.

She would find refusing strange. It's a working arrangement that makes the team
more effective. That's most of what she thinks a team is.
