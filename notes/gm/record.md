## Session 1 — character creation (in progress)

Repo was empty at session start. No prior notes, no recap needed.

### Framing decisions

- **Party structure:** one PC, plus a GM-run companion NPC. Player picked this
  over solo or two-PC. The companion needs a voice and an agenda, not a stat
  block.
- **Campaign flavour:** player chose "surprise me" — explicitly handed the GM the
  choice, to be made *after* the character concept landed rather than before.
  Order matters: build the world around the character.

### The concept, in the player's words

> "I want to play a shirren mystic, but I want them to be a rebel. Basically,
> having broke from the hive mind they find it a violation. They hate that
> something took their free will from them, and feel an anger towards anything
> similar. They take it in a bit of an alien direction, due to the unfamiliar
> culture of existing in a hive mind. For example, they feel some anger towards
> the relationship between bosses and employees, because they ask the employees
> to do things they don't want to."

> "(Society probably wouldn't function if people never had to do things they
> didn't want to. This shirren hasn't exactly thought that through. And also it's
> less of an issue in shirren society, because they're inherently communal, and
> so, in a sense, *want* to do things that need to be done, which lets this
> position go unexamined more easily.)"

See `pc-intentions.md` — that file is the working version of this.

### Locked in

- **Ancestry:** shirren
- **Heritage:** Defiant (crit success on Will saves vs. Swarm-allied creatures
  *or any creature that is itself controlled*; Rebellious Defiance 1/day)
- **Class:** mystic
- **Connection:** Rhythm — primal tradition, trained in Performance, initial
  epiphany *anthem*

Player's reasoning for Rhythm, verbatim:

> "I'll go with Rhythm, because of the connection to Meyel, who is right up my
> alley, but I don't want to be a god's champion, because that's too close to the
> hive mind queen. I think the part of their mind that was a receiver for the
> queen's orders naturally fits tuning into the Song of the Spheres. But I think
> they're somewhat uncomfortable with it. Sometimes music is described as
> 'making people want to dance' and that sound close to compulsion in a bad way.
> But I think they may need to realize that it's not a compulsion like mind
> control, it's a compulsion like chocolate. People *already* want to dance, and
> music just gives them something to express that to."

### GM observations logged during creation, not yet used in play

- **Mystic Bond is a small consensual hive mind.** Bond up to 10 creatures; you
  always know their location, HP, and whether they're conscious. At 3rd level
  Group Chat adds telepathy across the bond. The rules require the creatures be
  **willing**. The PC's power is a hand-built version of the thing done to them,
  distinguished only by consent. This was raised with the player and they took
  it on board.
- **Defiant heritage's irony:** its critical-success benefit triggers against
  Swarm creatures *and against any creature that is itself being controlled*.
  The PC is at their strongest against other victims.
- ***Motivating ringtone*** (1st-rank connection spell, granted automatically)
  requires a comm unit **and the target's contact info**. The PC's best support
  spell cannot reach a stranger. Noted as thematically load-bearing.
- ***Charm*** is on the primal 1st-rank list and therefore available. The PC
  would refuse it. Flagged to the player as a position worth recording on the
  sheet rather than leaving as a silent absence. **Player has not responded to
  this yet — do not treat it as settled.**
