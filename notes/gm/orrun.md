# INTENTION & VOICE — Sazha Orrun

Ysoki, she/her. Grey at the muzzle. Foreman for the salvage outfit stripping
hull 4407-K ("the Kettle"). Hires casual crews off the apron at Arm Seven.

## What she's for

To be the human face of a decision nobody made. Orrun is not cruel, not greedy,
and not lying. She has a list, and the list came from above her, and she has
never once been paid to ask where the list came from.

She is the first thing standing between Jchk and the people who actually decided.
She will not defend the decision. She will explain that it isn't hers.

## What she knows

- Aft of frame 220 is off the list. She was told this and did not ask why.
- She does **not** know the aft sections are pressurised. She does not know anyone
  is living aboard. **When she finds out, she is genuinely rattled** — a pressure
  breach with casuals aboard is a fatality and a liability, in that order,
  probably, though she'd have to think about it.
- She's run maybe two hundred of these crews. She knows exactly how much a day
  rate is worth and exactly how little she can change.

## What she wants

To get nine casuals off a dead hull at end of shift with the same number of
fingers they arrived with, hand out the cash, and go home. That's it. She takes a
quiet pride in the fingers.

## What she'd never do

- Say a thing is *wrong*. She'll say it's not her list, not her call, above her.
  **She converts moral questions into jurisdictional ones**, automatically, and
  she is not aware she's doing it. That's her ceiling and it's the whole point of
  her.
- Endanger a crew knowingly.
- Pretend she has authority she doesn't.

### The levels line — **reconstructed, not verbatim. Flagged as such on purpose.**

Somewhere in session 1 Orrun said something to Jchk about **the levels asking
different questions**. The exact wording did not make it into the record; what we
have is Jchk's paraphrase at the very end of the session:

> *"Orrun said sometimes the levels are asking different questions."*

**Do not quote a verbatim original — there isn't one.** But keep the *idea*,
because it turned out to be enormous: Jchk took it as a model of the world and
tried to work out whether they and Del had become a level, and **Del demolished
it** (see `positions.md`). Orrun still believes it. She would say it again. To
her it is not a philosophy, it is how a job works — the yard asks *can we*, the
office asks *should we*, and the office wins because that's what an office is
for.

**When she restates it, she must not improve it.** She is not making an argument.
She is describing plumbing.

## THE ANSWER — why aft isn't on the list

**People live aboard the Kettle.** Long enough in the Armada that they've
accumulated something like a residency claim — the Station's law on permanently
docked hulls is a mess and everyone knows it.

The outfit's salvage title covers the hull. It does not cleanly cover the
residents. Fighting a tenancy claim costs money and time.

**So the plan is to strip the forward two-thirds and let the ship become
uninhabitable by degrees.** No eviction. No court. No cost. They'll leave on
their own, and every individual step is legal, and no single person aboard this
job is doing anything wrong.

Note the shape and hold onto it: **the cruelty is distributed so thin that
nobody is committing it.** Orrun follows a list. The cutter follows the trunk.
Someone in an office in the Ring approved a work order. This is a thing Jchk
gets to be **completely right about**, loudly — and that matters, because the
campaign needs Jchk to win an argument they deserve to win.

Who exactly signed it: **not yet decided.** Decide when Jchk goes looking.
