# POSITIONS

*Things characters committed to — out loud or by acting. What they said, to whom,
and what it cost. **Silence in front of something counts.** Consolidated from the
record; the record stays the primary source for how it happened.*

---

## Session 1

### Jchk — did not see the lashunta · **OPEN, uncosted**

In the pick-up queue, the foreman passed over a lashunta who had been in that
line at least four days, thin, coat too light. **Jchk did not notice.** Player,
verbatim:

> "I don't make the connection between being here all those days and their
> thinness. If they needed something, they would say something."

The shirren-communal assumption: need is broadcast; silence means sufficiency.
Cost so far: **nothing.** That is the problem. Should come back when it is
expensive, as a consequence and never as a lesson.

### Jchk — reported upward and waited · **broken, by them, in the same scene**

Sent the pressure hazard to whoever was in range, then stopped and held position
for the decision to come back down. Their politics are anti-hive; their reflexes
are not. **They violated their own anathema — submit to authority — by habit, in
a situation containing no authority at all.**

Then worked out there was nobody in the chain, walked forty metres, and delivered
it themselves. **First act of individual agency in the campaign, and it was
carrying a sentence.** Froze again immediately afterward.

### Jchk — would not instruct Del · **held, at a price**

Del twice offered to answer the mesh and then stood still, waiting to be given
the post. Jchk **refused to tell her to** and waited her out. Nothing happened.

**Cost, and it was real:** the only available translator stood idle while
somebody panicked behind a pressure door. Jchk then went and did it alone,
badly, by putting a voice in a stranger's head.

### Jchk — told the household the truth, unsoftened · **held**

> "Frame two-twenty and forward is scrap. Specialists will deal with this
> bulkhead."

Player: *"This is what I would tell a fellow worker, if they didn't know, and I
see no reason to treat this person any differently."* **To Jchk this is the
purest respect they have.** It caused about twelve seconds of panic hammering.

Repeated later and larger: Jchk disclosed **everything** to Sevi — shift length,
crew size, chain of command, who else knew — because *"everyone makes better
decisions when all information is available."*

### Jchk — could not say "want" · **held**

Asked directly by Del *"Do you want me to answer them?"*, Jchk answered:

> "Information exchange is not complete without a response. You can receive a
> response."

A yes, rendered as a protocol requirement. **Jchk cannot say they want a thing,
so they say the exchange is incomplete.** Del noticed — *"That's not what I
asked. But it'll do"* — and took it.

### Jchk — accepted Sevi's ask · **live commitment**

Agreed to try to find out **who signed the salvage order**. Framed to themselves
as a request for information, which is the one category of obligation Jchk has no
resistance to. **This is the campaign's first real hook and Jchk walked into it
through the only door they'd open.**

**Amended at the top of session 2, from the player's recap:** the "no resistance"
framing was too clean. Jchk is *struggling with what sort of obligation this
creates* — finding out that people live in a thing you were hired to take apart
does something, and Jchk does not know what, and has not settled it. What they
have settled is the narrow part: **they will do the thing that was actually
asked for.** The wider question of what else they owe these people is **live and
unresolved**, and it should stay that way. Do not have anyone name it for them.

### Orrun — took both positions inside one minute

Converted the moral question into a jurisdictional one, out loud, and heard
herself do it:

> "That's above me. That is genuinely, actually above me. I hire nine people off
> an apron and I keep them alive till end of shift. That's the whole of what I am
> on this job."

And then, walking away, without looking back:

> "I'm going to be up at the galley for five hours. I'm not counting heads till
> the lock."

**She deflected and she left the door open, and she knew she was doing both.**
This is the most she is capable of and it cost her something to do it.

### Del — took an unpaid post · **held**

Refused to walk away. Not from outrage — from trade. *"That's not a moral thing.
I did four years standing between a housing block and whatever showed up... There's
a thing here and nobody's on it. That's just an unstaffed post. It's sloppy."*

Then undercut her own framing with the shopping story, and did not notice:

> "He never asked me. Not once. He'd have died before he asked me. I just — I was
> there, and it needed doing, and I could reach the top shelf."

**She has the faculty Jchk lacks and treats it as unremarkable.**

And she named the terms she needs: *"I'm not asking you to be my supervisor... I'm
asking whether you want it. That's a different question."*

### Del — accepted that it stops with her · **held, and it cost her something**

Asked by Jchk whether the two of them were now "asking a different question," Del
rejected the framing — they aren't a level, they aren't on the chain, there is
nobody above them to lose the argument to — and then landed somewhere she clearly
had not been before:

> "So there's nobody to send it up to. It stops with us."
>
> "I've not had one stop with me before."

**This is Del's whole life in two sentences and it is not about Jchk at all.**
Twenty-odd years of being assigned, and the first decision that terminates in her
is one nobody is paying her for. She is not pleased about it. She is not
displeased either. She is somewhere she has no word for, which is exactly where
Jchk has been all session, arriving from the opposite direction.

**Do not resolve this into a friendship beat.** They are two people who have both
just discovered there is nobody above them, and neither has any idea what to do
with that.

### Fennow — closed it with generosity · **held, and it worked**

Stopped the cut, ordered nobody aft of frame 200, paid all nine the full day rate
including the standing-around, and said *"tell whoever spotted it they did exactly
right."* Then: *"We'll take it from here. That's a specialist job and it isn't
yours."*

**The generous act and the silencing act were the same act**, and there is no
sign he experiences the second one as happening.

### Sevi — asked, with an escape hatch · **held**

> "im not going to ask you to do anything. you dont know us and you came here to
> work"

> "if this costs you the job dont do it. i mean that. i know how that sounds
> coming from someone behind a door. i still mean it"

**She built the exact protocol Jchk requires in order to perceive a need at
all** — stated plainly, in complete sentences, with an explicit way to refuse —
and she arrived at it from caution, not kindness. She has learned that people who
feel trapped by you stop answering.
