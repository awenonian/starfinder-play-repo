# Absalom Station — what Jchk knows

*Player-facing. Things Jchk knows, or that people on the Station generally know.
Uncertainty is fine here and marked where it exists.*

---

## The Station

A space station floating in the empty orbit where the planet Golarion used to be,
before Golarion disappeared during the Gap. **Nobody knows who built it.** It
appeared sometime in those missing centuries and was simply there when history
started again. Its reactors are powered by the Starstone, which is also the most
powerful Drift beacon in existence — which is why every journey to Absalom is a
short one, and why everyone ends up here eventually.

It is the centre of interstellar trade and government in the Pact Worlds.
Hundreds of corporations and embassies. A population from everywhere.

### Districts

- **The Eye** — the shielded centre. Jatembe Park, corporate towers, the good
  neighbourhoods, the university and the solarian cosmonastery.
- **The Ring** — eclectic neighbourhoods and industrial districts wrapped around
  the Eye. Where most people actually live.
- **The Arms** — hangars and docks, jutting out of the Ring.
- **The Spike** — a warren of dilapidated industrial tunnels running down out of
  the Ring, intersecting with the **Ghost Levels**, which are dangerous and which
  people do not go into on purpose.
- **The Armada** — a permanent ragged swarm of ships docked in orbit, many of
  which have not moved in years.

## Where Jchk lives

The Spike, and further down than most. It is possible to be a recluse on the most
crowded station in the galaxy, as long as you are willing to live where the air
handling is bad.

## The Open Hand of Hylax

*Proposed — see the GM's note. Not yet confirmed canon.*

A mutual-aid house in the Ring for people who came out of something: indenture,
captivity, contracts, addiction — and ex-Swarm shirrens, of whom the Station
receives a steady trickle and for whom nobody else has made any arrangements at
all.

It is affiliated with the faith of **Hylax the Forever Queen**, goddess of
diplomacy, first contact, friendship and peace. Hylax was a mortal shirren hive
queen before she became a god. Her edicts are to build relationships, work toward
peace, and welcome others into your community. Her faith is entirely respectable.

The Open Hand takes that faith one step further than the mainstream church does.
Its members accept **placements** — work, lodging, and duties assigned by the
house rather than chosen by the member. The teaching is that the hard part of
freedom isn't danger, it's *deciding*, and that being spared the deciding is a
kindness.

**Jchk lived there for about a year after arriving, and left.**

Things Jchk knows for certain about the place:
- It fed them and housed them and asked nothing they could point to as a price.
- The people there meant it.
- Most of the others stayed. Most of them are fine.
- Jchk would rather have died than stay.

**Not settled:** how they got out, and what was said. That's yours to write.
