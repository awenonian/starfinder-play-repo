## Languages

**Common, Shirren, Trinary, Vesk**, + one more from Intelligence (**OPEN**).

- **Trinary** — Aballon; anacites and androids. Beings manufactured to serve, and
  then abandoned by the First Ones who built them. Jchk has opinions.
- **Vesk** — the Veskarium's tongue. Note who actually speaks it: an empire of
  seven conquered planets, plus the pahtras of Pulonis, who fought a war of
  independence and are still listed among its speakers. Jchk did not learn Vesk
  to talk to vesk. They learned it because it's what the occupied were made to
  learn.

**Limited Telepathy** — communicate mentally with creatures within **30 feet**
that share a language with you. No access to their thoughts; carries no more
information than speech would.

## Class features

**Mystic Bond** — 10-minute activity, adjacent to the target, up to 10 other
**willing** creatures. You always know each bonded creature's rough distance and
direction, their current Hit Points, and whether they're conscious, unconscious,
dying, or dead. Doesn't work across planes (including in the Drift).

**Vitality Network** — capacity **10 HP** (6 + 4/level). Regains **4 HP** at the
start of each of your turns in combat. Fully refills when you Refocus.

**Transfer Vitality ◆** *(concentrate, healing, mystic)* — move up to **10 HP**
from the network into yourself or a bonded creature you can sense within 60 feet.

**Harmony (Rhythm)** — when you Transfer Vitality, extend the duration of any
active rhythm epiphany by 1 round.

> Read that pairing again: healing someone and sustaining the song are the *same
> gesture*. The act of caring for an ally is what keeps the music going.

## Spells — primal tradition

**Spell slots:** 3 × 1st rank per day. **Focus pool:** 1 point.

**Cantrips (5)** — cast at will, auto-heightened to half your level rounded up.

- **Summon instrument** *(connection cantrip)* — 1 action, 1 hour. A handheld
  instrument appears in your grasp. **It plays only for you.**
- **Electric arc** — 2 actions, 30 ft, 1 or 2 creatures, 2d4 electricity, basic
  Reflex. Your workhorse.
- **Guidance** — 1 action, 30 ft, 1 creature. +1 status bonus to one attack roll,
  Perception check, save, or skill check before your next turn. *The target
  chooses when to spend it.* Offered, not imposed.
- **Stabilize** — 1 action, 30 ft, 1 dying creature. It loses the dying
  condition (still unconscious at 0 HP).
- **Detect magic** — 2 actions, 30-ft emanation.

**1st-rank repertoire (3)**

- **Motivating ringtone** *(connection spell)* — 2 actions, 120 ft, targets **1
  comm unit**. *Requires: you have a comm unit, and the target's contact info.*
  The owner regains **1d8+4 HP**, gains +5 ft status bonus to all Speeds, and +1
  status to saves against fear, for 1 minute while the ringtone keeps playing.
  Auditory, emotion, healing, **subtle**.
- **Heal** — 1 to 3 actions. 1 action: touch, 1d8. 2 actions: 30 ft, **1d8+8**.
  3 actions: 30-ft emanation, all living and undead in it.
- **Sonic scream** — 2 actions, 15-ft cone, 1d8 sonic, basic Fortitude; a
  creature that fails is also sickened 1. *"A cathartic scream."*

**Focus spell — Anthem ◆** *(initial epiphany; concentrate, emotion, focus,
manipulate, mental, mystic)* — 60-foot emanation, sustained up to 1 minute.
All **bonded** creatures in the area gain **+1 status to attack rolls and damage
rolls**, and +1 status to saves against fascinated and confused.

> Anthem only helps people who agreed to be in the bond. The spell cannot be
> imposed on a stranger. Jchk had to ask, and someone had to say yes.

## Feats and heritage abilities

**Defiant shirren (heritage)**
- When you roll a **success** on a Will save against an effect created by a
  kucharn creature, a creature you know is Swarm-allied, **or any creature that
  is itself being controlled**, you get a **critical success** instead.
- **Rebellious Defiance ◆** *(mental, once per day)* — decry one foe within 100
  feet you can see as a tyrant or villain, and prepare to defy them even unto
  death. +1 circumstance bonus to attack rolls against that foe, and +1
  circumstance bonus to saves against that foe's attacks, spells, and effects,
  for 1 minute.

**Communalism ⟲** *(ancestry feat; concentrate, fortune, shirren; once per day)*
— **Trigger:** an ally within 10 feet fails a skill check that took 3 actions or
fewer. **Effect:** you broadcast encouragement or pertinent information into
their mind. They reroll and take the better result.

**Dubious Knowledge** *(background skill feat)* — when you **fail** a Recall
Knowledge check, you learn one true piece of information **and one erroneous
one**, and you have no idea which is which.

## Edicts and anathema

**Edicts**
- Ask before you act on someone.
- Offer people a way to refuse you.
- Say it out loud when you see someone being made to obey.

**Anathema**
- Bond an unwilling creature.
- Give an order.
- Submit to authority.

*Player cut a proposed edict about giving away what you have: selflessness is
not a settled question for Jchk, and an edict would have made it one. Left out
deliberately — this is an open tension, not an oversight.*

## On the age

26 is Jchk's **estimate**. The Swarm does not record when an individual began,
because there are no individuals to record. Every ex-Swarm shirren arrives in the
galaxy having to pick a number, and most of them pick one that makes the
paperwork go through.
